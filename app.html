Max humidity = highest room humidity before the glass reaches the dew point (mould-safe value in brackets, at 80% on the surface).
Dew point by the Magnus formula (a 17.625, b 243.04). Glass surface = room - U x 0.13 x (room - outside), with 0.13 m²K/W the standard interior surface resistance. U-values are typical centre-of-glass figures (5.8, 2.8, 1.1, 0.8 W/m²K); frames, edges and air movement make real edges colder, so treat results as a guide. 80% surface humidity is a common mould-risk criterion.
